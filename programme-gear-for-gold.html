PROGRAMMES / GEAR FOR GOLD

Academy-driven. Coach-led. Athlete-centric.

Gear for Gold strengthens athlete-centric academies across India by supporting them with sports science, infrastructure, technology, and performance systems to drive long-term excellence.

Gear for Gold, an initiative of the Infosys Foundation, is designed to build stronger, athlete-centric academies across India. By using GoSports' Academy Development Model, the programme empowers academies, enables coaches, and drives athlete development for long-term sporting excellence.

Academy-Driven Coach-Led Athlete-Centric
About the partnership

A flagship collaboration

Gear for Gold is a flagship partnership between Infosys Foundation and GoSports Foundation, designed to strengthen athlete-centric academies across India. Building on the Girls for Gold initiative, the programme now supports male, female, and para-athletes across Olympic disciplines.

"Infosys Foundation and GoSports Foundation share a joint passion for strengthening the sports ecosystem in India. We are delighted with the progress we have made together in nurturing exceptional youngsters across sporting disciplines."
Sumit VirmaniGlobal Chief Marketing Officer, Infosys; Trustee, Infosys Foundation

Interventions

Pillars of the Academy Development Model

01

Athlete development pathway

A structured system to identify, train, progress, and transition athletes across levels.

02

Coaching & support ecosystem

A tech-driven, integrated support ecosystem around athletes training at the academy.

03

Infrastructure & sports science

International-standard facilities and performance centres built for athlete and coach development.

04

Governance & programme management

Clear governance frameworks that drive decision-making, accountability, and effective delivery.
