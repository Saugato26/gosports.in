How we're funded

Three kinds of backing

GoSports Foundation is funded through a mix of corporate CSR partnerships, philanthropic and foundation giving, and government/federation collaboration — each tied to specific, measurable programmes rather than general operating costs.

Corporate CSR

Multi-year partnerships funding named programmes (e.g. Gear for Gold, funded by Infosys Foundation).

Foundations & philanthropy

Grants supporting athlete development, research, and community sport initiatives.

Federations & government

Commissioned work, policy collaboration, and knowledge-partnership engagements.

Sustaining the system

Fund the system, not just a season

Most of our funding is tied to a named programme — which is exactly what donors should expect. But every programme depends on capacity that no single programme budget covers. Unrestricted gifts to our operating fund go toward:

Research and the SAPA Stack Framework — the studies and toolkits every programme is built on.

Cross-academy technology and performance-tracking systems shared across programmes.

National convenings and practitioner capacity-building across the sector.

The staff and monitoring & evaluation systems that make every impact number on this site real.

This is a new giving category we'd recommend introducing alongside named-programme funding — not one GoSports currently runs. If adopted, it's worth defining a threshold (a founding-partner tier, for instance) the way many peer foundations do.
