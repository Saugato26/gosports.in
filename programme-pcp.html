PROGRAMMES / PCP

Para Champions Programme

Holistic performance support for India's Para Athletes, since 2015 — incepted with founding partner IndusInd Bank.

Executive summary

PCP supports India's elite and emerging Para Athletes through holistic performance support while strengthening the broader Para sports ecosystem.

In FY 2025–26, PCP supported 40 athletes across 15 sports and 11 states, delivering 601 interventions spanning physiotherapy, nutrition, equipment, coaching, and competition exposure.

These efforts resulted in 229 medals, including 164 international, with highlights including Rakesh Kumar winning two gold medals at the Rome 2025 European Para Archery Cup. Several athletes are progressing toward the Asian Para Games 2026 and LA 2028 Paralympics.

Founding partner: IndusInd Bank (since 2015)

Athlete impact story

From the field

[ Photo: Himanshu Nandal — Para Swimming ]

Himanshu Nandal — Para Swimming

The story: Technical and structural challenges around body awareness, recovery, and stroke mechanics limited his progression, with limited access to specialised coaching.

The GSF impact: PCP supported a high-performance training stint at Loughborough University, plus remote strength and conditioning support, improving his technique and race readiness.

The result: Qualified for the World Championships, won three gold medals at the National Para Swimming Championships, and moved from World Rank 14 to 10 in the 100m backstroke.
