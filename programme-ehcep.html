PROGRAMMES / EHCEP

Equal Hue Cricket Excellence Programme

Scholarship and high-performance development for emerging women cricketers across India.

Executive summary

EHCEP supports emerging women cricketers in India through structured scholarships, high-performance training, and holistic athlete development — building a sustainable talent pipeline as athletes transition from junior to elite levels.

FY 2025–26 was a strong year, marked by improved athlete performance and programme maturity: 4 championship wins, 18 individual awards, 3 international representations, and participation in the Women's Premier League.

The programme supported 20 athletes across 14 states, creating a strong pan-India presence and contributing to the growth of women's cricket.

Athlete impact story

From the field

Tanmayee Behera — Cricket

The story: A torn finger ligament sustained mid-match, sidelining her and jeopardising her Inter-Zonal selection.

The GSF impact: She was referred to specialist sports physician Dr. Deepak Bhatia, with a focused rehabilitation plan of rest, medication, and structured physiotherapy, alongside continuous monitoring.

The result: Despite the injury, she scored a match-winning century and won Player of the Match — then went on to make her List-A debut for East Zone, scoring 47 runs.
